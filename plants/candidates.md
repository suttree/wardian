# Plant Candidates

The display favours compact ornamental species over edible herbs. The plant should feel specimen-worthy when seen alone.

## First-Trial Preference

Start with foliage before flowers. Foliage is more likely to remain attractive over time and better supports the project goal of slow observation.

Strong first candidates:

- Coleus
- Baby's Tears
- Selaginella
- Small fern
- Moss trial

## Foliage Candidates

### Coleus

Why it fits:

- Strong leaf colour and pattern.
- Fast visual feedback.
- Easy to propagate.
- Can feel botanical and theatrical under good light.

Risks:

- May grow too quickly or become leggy.
- Needs pruning to remain exhibit-like.

## Flowering Candidates

Flowering plants may be better once the vessel, light, and care rhythm are understood.

- Sweet Alyssum
- Violas
- Miniature Snapdragons
- Lobelia
- Miniature Zinnias

## Selection Questions

- Does the plant remain attractive outside its peak?
- Are the roots visually interesting or at least acceptable?
- Does it tolerate passive hydroponics?
- Can it be kept compact?
- Does it suit a single-specimen display?
- Does it reward close inspection?
