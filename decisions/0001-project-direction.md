# Decision 0001: Start With A Single Living Exhibit

Date: 2026-07-04

## Decision

Begin with one specimen, one vessel, and one light rather than designing a full cabinet immediately.

## Context

The project has a strong aesthetic direction: a living museum exhibit inspired by Wardian cases, natural history collections, apothecary glass, library displays, and quiet technology.

The riskiest questions are not cabinet construction questions. They are display questions:

- Can a passive hydroponic specimen remain beautiful?
- Can visible roots and water be maintained without algae taking over?
- Can the lighting feel warm, domestic, and museum-like while supporting growth?
- Can the care ritual feel pleasant rather than fussy?
- Can one plant feel curated enough to justify the larger project?

## Consequences

The first prototype should stay deliberately small.

The project should gather observations, photos, materials research, and decisions before committing to furniture, automation, or multiple compartments.

Future technology should be introduced only when it protects the illusion of a living cabinet, improves care, or deepens observation.
