# Prototype 001: Single Specimen

## Purpose

Establish the core aesthetic and daily ritual before investing in a larger cabinet.

The prototype should answer whether a single passive hydroponic specimen can feel like a living museum object in the home.

## Success Criteria

After 30 days, the display should:

- still look intentional and beautiful
- keep the plant alive or produce useful evidence of why it failed
- maintain acceptable water clarity
- avoid obvious algae, odour, or mess
- have lighting that feels warm and exhibit-like
- be easy enough to inspect and maintain
- photograph well without cropping out important compromises

## Constraints

- No cabinet.
- No pumps.
- No elaborate automation.
- No visible grow-light purple.
- No visible generic plastic if it can be avoided.
- No major woodworking.

## Candidate Setup

- Glass laboratory-style vessel.
- Net pot or discreet plant support.
- LECA or another inert medium.
- Passive nutrient solution.
- Compact ornamental plant.
- Warm-white full-spectrum LED mounted or positioned above.
- Small label, ideally brass, paper, or museum-style card.

## First Questions

- Does clear glass create algae too quickly?
- Can the roots remain attractive as they grow?
- How warm can the light appear while still supporting growth?
- How much maintenance does the vessel require each week?
- Is the plant compelling enough as a single specimen?
- Does the display feel like a curated object rather than a plant pot?
