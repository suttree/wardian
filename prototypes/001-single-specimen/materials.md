# Prototype 001 Materials

This file tracks candidates and actual choices for the first single-specimen prototype.

## Vessel

Aim for laboratory or apothecary language rather than a normal flower pot.

Search terms:

- borosilicate glass vase
- hydroponic propagation vase
- apothecary jar
- tissue culture jar
- chemistry beaker
- wide-mouth laboratory vessel
- glass specimen jar

Selection notes:

- Wide enough mouth for cleaning.
- Stable enough not to feel precarious.
- Clear enough to show roots and water.
- Not so large that one small plant feels lost.
- Easy to top up without disturbing the display.
