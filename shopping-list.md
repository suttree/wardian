# Shopping List

This list is for Prototype 001: one shelf, one vessel, one light, one plant.

Preference: Amazon UK for small components, IKEA for shelf/display furniture.

## Buy First

These are the parts needed to build the first single-specimen display.

## 1. Shelf / Display Base

### Best first choice: IKEA wall shelf

Chosen recommendation:

- [IKEA BERGSHULT shelf with PERSHULT brackets](https://www.ikea.com/gb/en/search/?q=BERGSHULT%20PERSHULT)

Look for:

- BERGSHULT shelf
- PERSHULT brackets
- GRANHULT brackets
- SANDSHULT brackets

Why:

- simple
- inexpensive
- easy to change later
- keeps the first prototype from becoming a furniture project

Preferred finish:

- white shelf with brass/wood details
- wood shelf with black or brass brackets
- avoid glossy plastic finishes

### Alternate: IKEA IVAR

Chosen future-cabinet recommendation:

- [IKEA IVAR pine shelving](https://www.ikea.com/gb/en/search/?q=IVAR%20shelving%20unit%20pine)

Useful if the project quickly wants multiple specimens.

Look for:

- IVAR pine shelving unit
- IVAR shelves
- IVAR side units

Why:

- solid pine
- modular
- stainable
- easy to adapt into a cabinet-like object later

## 2. Vessel

Choose one vessel first. The vessel should look beautiful empty.

Chosen recommendation:

- [1000ml borosilicate low-form glass beaker](https://www.amazon.co.uk/s?k=1000ml+borosilicate+low+form+glass+beaker)

### Best first search

Amazon UK search terms:

- `1000ml borosilicate glass beaker`
- `600ml borosilicate glass beaker`
- `wide mouth borosilicate glass jar`
- `glass apothecary jar wide mouth`
- `glass cylinder vase 15cm`
- `laboratory glass beaker 1000ml`

Selection notes:

- 600ml to 1000ml is a good first range.
- The mouth should be wide enough to clean.
- A low-form beaker may look more stable than a tall narrow one.
- Avoid vessels with lots of decorative moulding.

Initial recommendation:

- 1000ml borosilicate low-form beaker.

It has the right laboratory language, enough water volume, and should be easy to clean.

## 6. Light

The light should feel like museum lighting before it feels like grow equipment.

Chosen recommendation:

- [SANSI E27 white full-spectrum grow bulb, 10W to 15W](https://www.amazon.co.uk/s?k=SANSI+E27+full+spectrum+grow+light+bulb)

Amazon UK search terms:

- `SANSI E27 grow light bulb full spectrum`
- `E27 full spectrum grow light bulb warm white`
- `white full spectrum grow light bulb E27`
- `full spectrum grow light clamp timer white`
- `plant spotlight grow light warm white`

Initial recommendation:

- E27 full-spectrum white grow bulb, ideally in the 10W to 15W range.
- Pair it with a simple fixture rather than a visible multi-arm grow lamp.

Avoid:

- purple/red-blue lights
- obvious four-arm clamp grow lights
- ring lights
- cold clinical desk lamps

## 7. Lamp Holder / Fixture

Chosen recommendation:

- [Black metal adjustable E27 clamp spotlight](https://www.amazon.co.uk/s?k=black+metal+E27+adjustable+clamp+spotlight)

Amazon UK search terms:

- `E27 clamp lamp black metal`
- `E27 pendant lamp holder black`
- `E27 spotlight clamp lamp`
- `adjustable spotlight E27 black`

Initial recommendation:

- black metal E27 clamp or spotlight fixture.

Notes:

- The bulb matters more than the fitting at this stage.
- The fixture should be adjustable.
- Cable routing should be considered part of the display.

## 8. Timer

## 10. First Plant

Chosen recommendation:

- Coleus cutting, ideally taken from an existing plant or bought locally as a small plant.

Best first options:

Initial recommendation:

- 1000ml borosilicate beaker

The goal is not to buy the cabinet. The goal is to create Specimen LC-001.
