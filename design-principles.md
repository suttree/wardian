# Design Principles

## Curation Over Cultivation

The display exists to present a living specimen with care. Growth matters, but it is not the only goal. The object should be judged by atmosphere, ritual, legibility, and long-term beauty as much as plant performance.

## Each Vessel Is An Exhibit

Every specimen should feel individually chosen and displayed. Avoid visual clutter. One vessel should be able to stand alone as a complete object.

## Visible Life, Quiet Technology

Roots, water, glass, leaves, and labels should be visible. Wires, sensors, timers, pumps, and power supplies should recede.

Technology is welcome when it supports the museum illusion. It should not announce itself as consumer electronics.

## Light Is The Primary Material

Lighting should do more than keep the plant alive. It should create focus, depth, shadow, and a sense of importance.

Prefer warm-white full-spectrum lighting over purple grow lights. Visitors should notice the specimen before they notice the lamp.

## Materials Should Age Well

Prefer warm woods, glass, brass, blackened steel, antique bronze, ceramic, stone, and paper.

Avoid glossy plastics, visible generic electronics, and anything that makes the object feel disposable.

## Slow Observation

The project should reward returning attention. It should make small changes visible: new roots, water level, leaf posture, condensation, flowering, decline, pruning, rotation, and care.

## Domestic Museum, Not Lab Bench

Laboratory language is useful, but the final feeling should be domestic, warm, and contemplative. It can borrow from scientific display without becoming clinical.

## Preserve The Mystery

The display should not explain itself too eagerly. Labels and interfaces should be restrained. A good specimen can carry a little ambiguity.

## Living Archive

The project can carry a quiet narrative layer: each plant as a catalogued specimen in a private living collection. Accession numbers, provenance, handwritten notes, and seasonal observations should deepen the object without turning it into overt set dressing.
