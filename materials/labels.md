# Labels

Labels should make the display feel curated without over-explaining it.

## Desired Qualities

- small
- calm
- legible
- museum-like
- physically beautiful
- easy to update when specimens rotate

## Candidate Label Styles

## Brass Label Holder

Pros:

- Strong cabinet and archive association.
- Ages well.
- Works with wood and glass.

Cons:

- Can become too decorative if oversized.

## Cream Museum Card

Pros:

- Simple and flexible.
- Easy to replace.
- Good natural history language.

Cons:

- Needs careful typography or handwriting.
- Can warp near moisture.

## Black Card With White Ink

Pros:

- Strong contrast.
- Could feel specimen-like and theatrical.

Cons:

- May become too dramatic for the quiet mood.

## E-Paper Label

Pros:

- Future-facing without glowing.
- Supports changing species data or care state.

Cons:

- Risks making the technology too visible.
- Should only be added once the physical display language is established.

## Possible Label Content

- common name
- Latin name
- accession-style number
- date introduced
- source
- short observation

Example:

```text
COLEUS
Coleus scutellarioides
Specimen 001
Introduced: 2026-07-04
```
